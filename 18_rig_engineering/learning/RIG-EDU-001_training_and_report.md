# RIG-EDU-001 — Engineering training and report evidence

Aim: demonstrably learn design-to-build-to-test, not use every machine on one
part. A training coupon is a valid separate skill artifact when that process
is unnecessary for the final rig. Obtain staff approval and record only
completed work. No fake certification, fabricated results or portfolio claims.

| Competency | Work product | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|---|
| Systems engineering | stakeholder/requirements/ICD, design review | revisioned decisions and reviewer feedback |
| Mechanical CAD | assembly, datums, detailed drawings | native files, STEP, drawings, tolerance analysis |
| CNC and CAM | approved coupon or carrier and toolpath review | setup/process record, actual inspection |
| Manual milling/drilling/cutting | bracket or coupon | safety training, actual part and dimensional results |
| Lathe | locator/spacer or supervised coupon | turned feature inspection and fits rationale |
| Laser cutting | approved duct mockup/label panel | flat patterns, kerf/fit study with data |
| Additive manufacturing | unpowered sensor-positioning prototype | slicing file, material/process/fit record |
| Woodworking | independent training coupon or cool-zone mockup | supervised learning record, no hot-zone structural assumptions |
| Metrology | measurement of dimensions and repeatability | tool/ref ID, uncertainty and measured values |
| Electrical engineering | reviewed circuit, harness, terminal schedule | actual components, CAD schematic/approved inspection |
| Oscilloscope | reference waveform/probe verification | actual setup, bandwidth/attenuation/ground review, saved trace |
| Instrumentation/DAQ | calibrated temperature/V/I/cooling chains | configuration, reference checks, lag and time sync |
| Thermal chamber | approved loaded mapping and environmental assessment | chamber spec, measured uniformity/stability, heat-load permission |
| CFD and FEA | actual frame/duct/hot-zone design studies | boundary conditions, mesh convergence, comparisons to experiment |
| Controls/data | supervised state machine, logging and alarms | synthetic logic tests, separately approved real tests |
| Commissioning | rig acceptance and configuration | requirement evidence, NCR, final restrictions |

## Report: Part I — standalone rig engineering
1. Purpose and stakeholder needs; literature/design principles.
2. System architecture, trade-off decision and requirement traceability.
3. Standards applicability and risk reduction, not conformity claims.
4. Detailed mechanical design: loads, FEA, GD&T, thermal expansion, DFM/DFA,
   CAD/engineering drawings and as-built differences.
5. Thermal and airflow design; heat paths, CFD, chamber interface.
6. Electrical architecture and protection; circuit reviews, actual parts.
7. Instrumentation, control/data architecture and uncertainty.
8. Manufacturing methods, process planning, first-article inspection,
   deviations/engineering changes, costs and actual fabrication evidence.
9. Verification, commissioning, calibration, acceptance and limitations.
10. Skills and lessons learned, reproducibility and maintenance.

Part II — EBT scientific experiments: Q0/R01 protocol, independent remounts,
boundary qualification, B0–B3 comparison and uncertainty, positive/negative
results and limitations. Keep rig acceptance distinct from scientific claims.

Maintain evidence diary: date; objective; training scope and authorization;
equipment model/serial where permissible; actual task; CAD/tool versions;
measurements and uncertainty; photos only if permission; issue and resolution;
reviewer feedback; competence status (observed/trained/supervised/permitted
as supported). Never publish personal documents or confidential lab details.
