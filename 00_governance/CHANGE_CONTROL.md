# EBT change-control rules — active baseline 1.0

This baseline is controlled, **not frozen**. EBT v0.1 technical evidence is retained. The thesis question, scope, standards matrix, equipment plan, procedures and schedule are versioned independently.

1. Open `EBT-CR-###` with problem/opportunity, supporting evidence, proposed change, alternatives and impacts on safety, cost, experiments, controls, reproducibility, scientific validity and publication.
2. Identify affected requirements, tests, scripts and document revisions; perform a side-by-side impact comparison.
3. Approve/conditionally approve/reject/defer with date and decision-maker. Unresolved issues remain tracked rather than initiating an entire new plan.
4. Update impacted documents, register amendment, verify tests and add `CHANGELOG` entry. Preserve previous version and raw experimental measurements.
5. After confirmatory design lock (G3), any change affecting eligibility/exclusions/models/holdouts requires a dated protocol amendment; distinguish exploratory/reanalyzed results from preregistered confirmatory analysis.

**Versioning:** 1.0.x editorial only; 1.x substantive methodology or scheduling within core objective; 2.0 research pivot. `EBT-R01-TP` and software have their own revisions/tags.

**Weekly operating rhythm:** 15-minute review of open tasks, blockers, risks and spend. Every milestone: a reviewer-style evidence review. No test to be energized until actual facility authorization and local safety review.
