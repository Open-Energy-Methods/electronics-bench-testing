# Safety, provenance and publication boundaries

- This is an independent research planning and synthetic-feasibility package, not an accredited testing service, completed thesis, KTH-approved research project, verified physical model, or journal publication.
- Experimental work is **not authorized** by inclusion in this repository. Facility-specific safety and equipment permissions are mandatory.
- No mains, high-voltage, unsafe battery, or unattended heating experiments are authorized by these draft protocols.
- All present research datasets are labeled synthetic. No actual component/junction temperatures have been measured.
- Package surface temperature must never be represented as directly measured semiconductor junction temperature.
- In-house instrument comparison is not automatically an accredited or metrologically traceable calibration.
- Do not upload confidential KTH documentation, proprietary equipment information, serial numbers tied to restricted systems, access credentials, certificates with personal identity numbers, or lab photographs without permission.
- Public release of real measurements requires provenance, raw-data integrity checks, facility permission and rights review.
