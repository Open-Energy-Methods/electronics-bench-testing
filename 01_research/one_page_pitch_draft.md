# Research concept (INTERNAL, not yet sent)

**Title:** When Surface Temperatures Mislead: Observability and Cross-Boundary Validation of Compact Electrothermal Models for Low-Voltage Power-Electronics Cooling.

**Motivation:** A thermal model can fit a package temperature without uniquely identifying an internal thermal path; cooling-boundary transfer and reassembly further constrain reproducibility. Related work already addresses transient thermal identification; the contribution must be differentiated, not presumed.

**Research objectives:** (1) quantify observability of internal state hypotheses under realistic surface-sensor uncertainty; (2) test predictive transfer between independently measured cooling conditions; (3) determine the effect of mounting/interface variability on model predictions.

**Method:** Design a protected low-voltage resistive pilot then semiconductor fixture; verify electrical V·I and temperature channels; sample temperature transients at defined package/sink/ambient points; predeclare blocked independent remounts and holdout configurations; compare compact thermal networks and optional spatial models; quantify measurements and model uncertainty; preserve raw data and code for replication.

**Work complete:** Python 3-node synthetic thermal model with seven passing unit/physics tests; fixed-seed synthetic holdouts; initial prior-art overlap matrix and draft campaign. **No physical experiments.**

**Main need from potential academic collaborator:** technical critique of novelty, junction-temperature measurement strategy, suitable instrumentation and controlled thermal-interface repeatability. Not requesting attribution or institutional branding by default.

**Deliverable:** open data/code and qualified physical benchmark; manuscript if the differentiated contribution and evidence justify submission. All laboratory work needs explicit facility authorization.
