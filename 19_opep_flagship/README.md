# OPEP — Open Physical Engineering Platform
## Flagship portfolio programme | concept baseline 0.1 | 18 November 2026

OPEP is a proposed independent physical-engineering design, manufacture,
measurement and validation programme. EBT-RIG-001 is its first modular test
dock; the existing EBT Q0/R01 study is the first electrothermal research
application. Existing EBT governance, raw schema and safety gates remain
authoritative for that campaign. Do not repurpose their roles or results.

The common asset is a controlled engineering workflow, repeatable specimen
and fixture interfaces, borrowed/qualified instrumentation and a data system.
It is not a universal powered bench or one machine intended to contain
machining, laser cutting and every future experiment. Workshop manufacture,
clean metrology, powered electronics, mechanical loading and chamber tests
must occur in their appropriate authorized areas.

## Baseline decisions
Start with an unpowered precision interface, training coupons and measurement
system characterization. Develop M04/M05 on approved low-energy references
in parallel when permitted. Complete initial electrothermal cassette only
after real G2/RD3–RD5 review. Future load, rotating, fluid and environmental
modules require separate safety/measurement/fixture review. No pressure,
mains, heavy loading, hazardous materials or unattended powered operation in
the first baseline. Do not add machinery merely to increase portfolio count.

No actual instrument models or calibration, machine permission, component
procurement, physical measurements, certification, manufacturing release,
laboratory sponsorship or powered authorization have been verified here.
User-reported capabilities are inputs for survey. Budget and every physical
acceptance tolerance are open pending real survey, design and staff review.
