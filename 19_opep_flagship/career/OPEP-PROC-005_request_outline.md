# OPEP shared-equipment request outline (DRAFT, not sent)

Start with the room's purchasing manager and board. Public guidance:
https://wiki.makerspace.se/Hej
https://wiki.makerspace.se/Ink%C3%B6psansvariga
https://wiki.makerspace.se/Styrelsen_2026
https://wiki.makerspace.se/doc/maskiner-och-inventarier-8991ffd97b

Ask for inventory clarification, existing loanable equipment and interested
members before requesting a purchase. The published purchasing page refers
to 2023 budgets and must be reconfirmed. Do not order, donate or transport
new shared assets without prior approval.

Candidate first shared investments (illustrative priorities, no quotes):
1. Dimensional inspection (dial indicators, holders, reference artefacts).
2. Reusable thermometry and low-range pressure/flow measurement.
3. Acoustic measurement microphone and calibrated dynamic measurement
   chain or loan; distinguish screening equipment from traceably calibrated.
4. Approved small cold-air source and reusable duct components.
5. Commercial process-air heater only after measured need, host approval,
   ventilation/power/protection review and all-in quote. Seek industrial surplus
   or external lab rather than a private heater purchase first.

Proposal fields: owner/steward, current equipment gap, multiple member use
cases, alternative/loan search, documented specification, three comparable
quotes if feasible, VAT/shipping/calibration/consumables/maintenance and
installation, storage footprint, training/risk review, public member access,
acceptance and maintenance procedures. Shared investment decisions are made
by the association. No request sent or equipment assigned.
