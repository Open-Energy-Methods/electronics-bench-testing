**Recorded:** 2026-11-05, KTH Canvas course [32080](https://canvas.kth.se/courses/32080), from the signed-in course front page. **Access state:** course content visible; safety introduction and machine permission NOT verified. **Scope:** mechanical project only. This record is **NOT** a permit to fabricate or conduct powered testing.

## Actual Canvas instructions (not a proposed EBT procedure)

The workshop describes itself as **project-based**, requiring participants to work on a defined project with a goal and timeline. Project groups are managed under **People / Persons**; join a relevant group according to staff instructions, rather than assuming a private independent project has already been accepted.

**To book the initial workshop introduction:**
1. Open [Canvas Calendar with course 32080 selected](https://canvas.kth.se/calendar?include_contexts=course_32080), and ensure **EECS Student Workshop** calendar visibility is enabled.
2. Select **Find Appointment**, then choose an available introduction. Course front page also shows appointment groups at the bottom, but this is not a reliable vacancy counter and does not work in the Canvas mobile app.
3. The front page's dated listing (viewed 5 November) included **5 November 2026 17:00–18:00**, [appointment group 23312](https://canvas.kth.se/appointment_groups/23312), and **6 November 2026 16:00–17:00**, [appointment group 23356](https://canvas.kth.se/appointment_groups/23356). **Neither is booked or confirmed free**; use live Find Appointment before travel. If no future appointments appear, Canvas says more will be added.

**After booking:** attend the general safety introduction, then complete the **post-introduction quiz**, download/sign/re-upload the **safety acknowledgement** in its Canvas assignment.

**Additional task controls:** for **CNC mill, PCB mill, lathe, manual mill, pillar drill, laser cutter, chemicals or pressurized gas equipment**, download the respective **official risk assessment PDF** from Canvas Files, review/modify where appropriate, sign and upload under the corresponding Canvas assignment. The mechanical machines also require a separate **practical introduction by Sebastian or Shamsheer**. Do not treat the general induction or form submission as proof of machine qualification. Staff control the equipment's access code and eligibility.

**Incident requirement per Canvas:** notify the workshop safety responsible and report using the official KTH incident system. Follow the actual site induction and contact instructions, not a copied contact list in an open repository.

## Project-specific route for EBT

1. **Bring a [one-page project brief](EBT-G2-012_project_brief.md)** defining a bounded mechanical deliverable and approximate timeline, not an application to operate a powered thermal rig.
2. **Ask what can be done first without machining:** inspect a received sample and check contact-face geometry with approved measuring tools under staff guidance. No sample receipt is currently confirmed.
3. **Select workshop processes only after discussing feasibility:** e.g. approved mechanical sensor brackets, external clamps or drilling **only if** site staff approve the part-specific drawing, training, tool and associated risk assessment. No hole depths/torque/fastener geometry are approved in EBT.
4. **Separate the electrical scope:** heater power tests, DAQ, electrical protection, fan-controlled thermal experiments and MOSFET tests require another specifically authorized electrical laboratory and site SOP.
5. Capture the actual introduction booking, attendance, quiz, safety agreement, official machine risk assessments and granted permissions **privately** in [the unfilled evidence record](EBT-G2-012_access_record_UNFILLED.json). Do not commit private staff contact details, appointment receipts, access codes or unredacted personal records to public GitHub.
6. Reevaluate the G2 checklist against physical permissions, not a Canvas enrollment or passing automated test.
