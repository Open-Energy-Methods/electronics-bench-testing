width, 40 mm height, **10 mm base thickness**, 100 mm length and
black anodization. Source PDF:
https://www.hod-electronics.nl/UserFiles/File/Shop/SK%2092-100-SA.PDF

permission for machining or a verified convection boundary. The
resistor's 20 W catalog rating at 25°C case is **not an experiment
power approval**.
