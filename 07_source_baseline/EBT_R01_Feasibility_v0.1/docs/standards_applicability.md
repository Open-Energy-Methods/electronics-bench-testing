# Standards applicability and claim control (v0.1)

These are **references**, not compliance certificates or blanket requirements. Full normative documents must be legally obtained and reviewed by responsible personnel before stating compliance.

| Standard or guidance | Proposed role | Current claim | Official reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISO/IEC 17025:2017 | Relevant laboratory competence, traceability, record and quality principles | Independent lab not accredited; guidance only | https://www.iso.org/standard/66912.html |
| JCGM 100:2008, including 2026 amendment and JCGM 101:2008 | Uncertainty budgets, propagation and Monte Carlo where appropriate | Planned; no real instruments assessed | https://www.bipm.org/en/committees/jc/jcgm/publications |
| ASME V&V 20-2009 (R2021) | Explicit model-versus-test validation variables and points; numerical and experimental uncertainty | Approach inspiration; no compliant V&V statement | https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/standard-for-verification-and-validation-in-computational-fluid-dynamics-and-heat-transfer |
| JEDEC JESD51 series, particularly JESD51-14 where applicable | Relevant device/package thermal test method and junction-to-case concepts | No JESD51 measurement performed; applicability TBD | https://doi.org/10.1109/STHERM.2011.5767204 |
| IEC 61010-1 | Safety considerations for electrical measurement and laboratory equipment | Equipment safety considered; not compliance tested | official IEC catalogue; review exact scope before use |

## Boundaries

- In-house verification/calibration must identify a traceability chain and uncertainty where those claims are made.
- KTH facilities cannot be described as accredited for this experiment without direct evidence.
- JEDEC thermometric definitions apply to particular devices and methods; surface temperature is not junction temperature.
- No destructive, mains-connected or high-energy battery tests in early-stage protocols.
