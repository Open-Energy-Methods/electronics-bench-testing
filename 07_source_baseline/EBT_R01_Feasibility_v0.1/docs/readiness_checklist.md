## Open before academic pitch claiming research results
- [ ] Full papers screened and novelty claim cross-checked against at least IEEE Xplore, Scopus/DiVA and reference snowballing.
- [ ] Primary device and electrical losses characterized with manufacturer data.
- [ ] KTH facility permission, access/card induction and energized testing arrangements confirmed.
- [ ] Instrument inventory with actual serial numbers, traceability evidence and stated uncertainty completed.
- [ ] Safety analysis for actual circuit and instrument connection approved.
- [ ] Independent cooling boundary measurement or qualified inference method chosen.
- [ ] Pilot measurement: resistive heater and package+sink channels, repeatability demonstrated.
- [ ] Junction-temperature strategy selected, or claims narrowed explicitly to surface temperatures.
- [ ] Mechanical remount and calibration/holdout designs finalized and frozen.
- [ ] Main experiment with physical measured runs completed.
- [ ] Scientific results externally critically reviewed.
- [ ] Data rights, photo permissions and coauthorship/affiliation clarified.

## Honest KTH outreach threshold

A **methodology advice meeting** can be justified now by the working demonstration and literature screening. A claim of **empirical research findings, paper-ready work, KTH institutional approval or novelty** must wait until the relevant checks are complete.
