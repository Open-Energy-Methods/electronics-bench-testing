# EBT-LIT-011 — Governance, originality and paper-positioning work (WP0/1)

**Purpose:** preserve the independent research thesis while finding out whether there is an evidence-based publishable contribution. **G1 OPEN.** Existing critical source grading in [EBT-LIT-003](../../01_research/EBT-LIT-003_critical_full_text_assessment_v0.3.md) is authoritative; do not silently elevate abstract-only papers to full-method review.

## Outputs required

**G0 internal planning acceptance:** timestamped decision acknowledging EBT-PMP-001 v1.0 as the unchanged governing baseline; read the current requirements, risks, decisions, target physical-vs-numerical claim boundaries. This v1.1 roadmap is a minor implementation revision; it does not reclassify synthetic results.

**G1 searchable source audit:** retain exact query string, date, database, inclusion criteria, accessed full text/page notes, method comparison, author/year/DOI and measured target for primary papers in IEEE Xplore, Scopus/Web of Science if institutional access is permitted, KTH DiVA and publisher sites. Continue the existing 14-source register and try to resolve access to eight restricted competitors rather than adding superficial bibliographic count. Deduplicate genuinely competing prior art.

**Claim-level comparison:** for each of RQ1/H1 observability, RQ2/H2 independently qualified boundary transfer and RQ3/H3 remount variability, state:
- what earlier work demonstrably established;
- what EBT proposes *differently* in independent qualification, prospective holdout, uncertainty, fixture remount and accessible instrumentation;
- which competing exact-method claims remain unverified due to paywalls;
- whether EBT can actually test the proposed difference with the intended sensors.

**Mandatory G1 output:** a one- or two-page originality decision with references: (A) experimental transfer/observability paper, (B) open benchmark/methods paper, or (C) independently citable technical report. An unchanged abstract claim is not enough for (A).

## Scientific correction checkpoints

- Testing only `T_case` and a sink sensor does **not** identify junction temperature absent separate validated sensitive thermometry.
- A predeclared scientific null/negative transfer result may be useful; it is not automatically original.
- A manufacturer's heat-sink thermal resistance listed by a distributor does not prove an independently measured boundary.
- If exact source/site geometry prevents transferable boundary characterization, revise RQ2 through controlled change, not selectively remove failed cases.

## Acceptance, owner, and fallback

**Owner:** current independent researcher. **Optional independent academic critique:** only if a qualified person agrees; no one is assigned without consent.
**Done when:** the existing evidence matrix is updated source-by-source, G1 decision and remaining uncertainty are recorded, and the introduction claims are calibrated accordingly.
**If novel contribution not demonstrated:** deliver software + methods + limitations and an honest technical report, while leaving any physical-data claim dependent on actual measurements.
